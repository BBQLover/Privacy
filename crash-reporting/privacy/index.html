Crash reporting

BBQ Daisuki Applications Crash Reporting Notice

1. Overview & Voluntary Submission

This crash reporting service is a voluntary tool designed to help us diagnose and fix technical issues in BBQ Daisuki applications. By submitting a report, you help us improve the software.

2. Information Collected

When you submit a report, it may include non-identifying technical data required to debug the crash, such as:

  • System & Hardware Info: Operating system, GPU, CPU, memory, and runtime environment.
  • Diagnostic Data: Crash logs, error codes, call stacks, and related diagnostic files.
  • User Input (Optional): Descriptions, screenshots, or comments provided manually by you.

3. Your Responsibility (Important)

Crash logs are generated automatically and are intended to contain only technical information. However, logs may occasionally capture temporary paths or text.

Please DO NOT include sensitive personal information (such as passwords, private credentials, or personal identification) in your comments or attachments. You are solely responsible for the content you choose to submit.

4. How We Use & Retain Data

Purpose: Data is used exclusively to investigate bugs, improve stability, and protect the service from abuse. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Retention: Reports are retained only as long as necessary to resolve software issues and will be periodically deleted or cleaned up.

5. Anonymity & Data Requests

Submitted reports are processed as anonymous technical diagnostics and are not linked to your personal identity. Because we do not track or store who submitted which report, we are unable to locate, modify, or delete specific crash reports upon individual request.

6. Contact

If you have questions regarding this notice, contact us at: privacy@bbqdaisuki.moe
